# Greyfall

The words for Aurum and the Atlas, one per thing. Docs, code and player text
use these; the rules for telling the story are in
[docs/world/reach.md](docs/world/reach.md).

## The world

**Aurum**:
The whole world; "gold" in the old kings' tongue.
_Avoid_: the Reach, the kingdom, the realm under one crown

**The Hearth**:
The warmth under Aurum.
_Avoid_: the core, the heart of the world, magic

**The old kings**:
The dead in the barrows. Never a living ruler.

**The Old Law**:
The kings' law against deep digging: "no deeper than a grave".

**The Broken Crown**:
The old kings' crown, split between the holds.
_Avoid_: the Shattering

**The Deep Cut**, **the Cut**:
The Citadel's last shaft, into the Hearth.

**The Cold Year**:
The first winter of the Grey.

**The Grey**:
The cold that takes: colour, then name, then will. Always capitalised; lower-case grey is only a colour.
_Avoid_: the plague, the rot (as its name), the Greying (code only)

**The taken**:
Those the Grey has kept. "Grey men" only in a child's mouth.
_Avoid_: zombies, undead, hollows

**The Grey Warlord**, **the Warlord**:
The Citadel's last lord, kept warm by his crown; the drain the Grey runs into.
_Avoid_: the Grey King, the Grey itself, his name

**The crown**:
The Warlord's crown of Hearth gold. Folk do not know what it is made of.
_Avoid_: the Hearth Crown (in folk mouths)

**The gnome-kings**:
The gnomes who went up to Sunhold with the gold. Never the gnomes below.

**The Order**:
The purple faith, broken three ways: the Keepers, the Stilled, the Pilgrims.
_Avoid_: the cult (folk may say it of the Stilled)

## The other folk

**The other folk**:
All the peoples who are not men, in Lanternport's polite voice.
_Avoid_: races, monsters, creatures, demihumans

**Gnomes**:
The gnomes of Brasscrag. Folk say tinkers.

**Goblins**:
Folk say diggers.

**Gnolls**:
Folk say dogs, the Grey's dogs.

**Fenfolk**, **lizard-folk**:
The people of Stillmere.
_Avoid_: lizardmen

**Trolls**:
Old folk say sleepers.

**The horned**:
The Citadel's oathsworn guard. Folk say bullmen.
_Avoid_: minotaurs (in player text)

**Bamboo-folk**:
The pandas of Brasscrag. Hats, said rudely.
_Avoid_: pandas (in player text)

**Finfolk**:
The sea people. Folk say sharks.
_Avoid_: sharkmen

**Imps**:
Folk say sparks.
_Avoid_: demons

**The masks**, **barrow-wardens**:
The hex shamans of Kingsrest.
_Avoid_: shamans, witch doctors

**Hex**:
The masks' craft, kept for the Old Law: a bolt cast at a foe, which slows what it bursts on.
_Avoid_: magic, spell, curse

## Places

**Tier**:
One of Aurum's three layers: the sky, the surface, the underneath.

**Land**:
A named place in the canon, such as Highward or Kingsrest, holding one or more areas. Each has its folder under `docs/world/`.
_Avoid_: region, zone

**Area**:
One map of a land, with its own id, such as Highward's `burg` or `keep`.
_Avoid_: level, stage

**Gate**:
A way between areas on the same tier: open, barred, locked, a shortcut or the high road.

**Lift**:
A way between tiers: both ways, down only, or a shortcut. A down-only lift is a drop.

**Shortcut**:
A gate or lift that opens from one side only and stays open, turning an area into a loop.

**Lanternport**:
The hub town, under Highward.
_Avoid_: Last Harbour, the Harbour (old names)

**Highward**:
The castle over Lanternport. Its wards are the Burg, the Ramparts, the Chapel Ward and the Keep.
_Avoid_: the Citadel

**The Burg Gate**:
Highward's front gate on the crown of Lanternport's hill, where the climb from the fire ends, and the way into the Burg.
_Avoid_: the Castle Gate

**Greyfall**:
The land of the crag and its rot marsh, where the Grey falls like ash.

**The Citadel**, **the Grey Keep**:
The fortress on Greyfall's crag, and the keep on its crown. Never Highward.

**The Pits**:
The Cut's works round the Crater, where goblins dug and the horned stood over them.
_Avoid_: the mines, the Diggings (Kingsrest's robbers)

**The Crater**:
The Deep Cut's mouth on Greyfall's Rotfield, fallen in on itself.

**The Counting House**:
The Citadel's pay office in the High City, where its ledgers are kept, and burned.

**The Throne Isle**, **the Throne Stair**:
The Citadel's seat at court in the sky, and its stair up.

**Sunhold**:
The gnome-kings' city in the sky. Folk say the sky city.
_Avoid_: the high city (that is Greyfall's High City)

**Brasscrag**:
The gnomes' rock.
_Avoid_: Tinker Crags (old name)

**The Diggings**:
Kingsrest's grave-robbers.

**The Robbers' Camp**:
Tobin's brother's camp in Graveroad.

## Play

**Hero**:
The player's character in docs and code; a survivor in narration. The folk never say its name.
_Avoid_: the wanderer, the chosen, the player character

**Tidewashed**:
What folk call the hero to its face, for the tide that brought it: one more washed up at Lanternport. Never more than that.
_Avoid_: the drowned, the chosen, the Unkindled

**Nameless**:
The hero's name when none was given. A save from before the class pick is named the Wanderer.

**Class**:
Who the hero came ashore as, picked at a new game's start: Warrior (with a shield), Lancer (who guards, and whose every thrust knocks back), Archer (who shoots from afar and pins with every third arrow) or Monk (who casts from afar and mends itself with every third prayer). The band can hold the hero's own kind.
_Avoid_: job, role, archetype

**The band**:
The hero's companions, up to three at once.
_Avoid_: party, squad

**Companion**:
One member of the band: Lancer, Archer or Monk.
_Avoid_: follower, ally, hireling

**Bonfire**:
A rest point that heals, saves and respawns the area. Folk say the fire.
_Avoid_: checkpoint

**Rest**:
Sitting at a bonfire.

**Gold**:
The only currency: levels, recruits, sword upgrades. Carried gold stays where the hero fell.
_Avoid_: souls, coins, money

**Boss**:
A keeper of a way out of a land, fought in its own arena.

**Notice**:
A note pinned up in the Lane House in Lanternport, read at its door: a foe, how many of it, where, and the gold paid at the door once that many have fallen. Nothing is taken up or tracked; read it, go, come back.
_Avoid_: quest, bounty, task, job

**The Grey Gate**:
The barred road into Greyfall, opened by the Horned Key and the Silk Key together.

**The Horned Key**, **the Silk Key**:
The Grey Gate's two keys, from the Barrow King and the Weaver. Always capitalised.

**The Ember Key**:
Highward's Keep Gate key, from the Ember Bull.
_Avoid_: the Troll Key, a key to the Grey Gate

**Dev mode**:
The game as its makers see it, behind a password: the hero plays on, with tools to go anywhere, talk to anyone and set the save.
_Avoid_: debug mode, cheat mode

**Free camera**:
Dev mode's view of a whole area map, loose of the hero, who stands still while it is on. Leaving it sets the hero down where it looks.
_Avoid_: whole map, dev view

**Dev panel**:
Dev mode's tools, in four tabs: Go, World, Hero and Screens.
_Avoid_: debug panel, dev menu

## The folk's lines

**Talk**:
One conversation with someone, a few short pages. Each talk plays their next one; the last repeats.
_Avoid_: dialogue tree, quest

**Remark**:
A talk that plays once, after the first talk there, when its flag has happened.

**Flag**:
A thing that has happened in the world, such as a boss slain or a gate opened.

**Questline**:
One person's run of talks and stations, moved on by flags. There is no quest log.

**Station**:
Where someone stands once its flags have happened. A station with no place means they are gone for good.

**Gift**:
An item a talk gives, free, the first time it is heard to the end.

**Stock**:
What a merchant sells; a line of it can wait on a flag.
