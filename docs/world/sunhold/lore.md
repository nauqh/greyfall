# Sunhold

The canon for this land. Where it and anything else disagree, this wins; the rules and the world's story are in [../reach.md](../reach.md).

## The land

The gnome-kings' city of isles, built on the Citadel's gold and raised by it:
enough warm gold, worked right, rises the way warm air does. For a hundred
years it took gold up the Throne Stair by the barge-load and never asked where
it came from. In return the Citadel's lords had the Throne Isle, their hall
at court, joined to their keep by the Throne Stair.

When the Cold Year came the lords below sent up for spears, and Sunhold shut
the Gilded Gate, barred the Gilded Span against the Throne Isle, and sent
none. Below they call it cowardice. It was arithmetic: the city floats on
warm gold, no gold has come up since the Cut, and it will not spend what
keeps it in the air. Its hoard is heaped in the Sun Hall to keep the isles
up, and the Sun Sentinel keeps the hall, behind its shield. It buys
Stiltwater's salt by the shipload to pack its foundations, and pays in gold
it can less and less spare.

It is cooling. Imps breed in its gold faster than the guard can burn them
out. The Throne Isle hangs dark off its east end, left to the Grey and the
imps, round a seat the Warlord does not sit in. The gnome-kings themselves
are not seen. Whether anyone rules up there is a question no one below can
answer.

- **Sunhold.** The gnome-kings' city of isles, gold behind a shut gate. Its
  yellow guard hold the walls and the span's foot, the Sun Sentinel keeps
  the Sun Hall, and the Throne Isle hangs dark off its east end. Its gold
  has not made it safe, only quiet.
