# Greyfall: the folk

Who lives here, and every line they say. How talks, flags and moving on work is in [../questline.md](../questline.md).

## Tally

Greets you: *"Not one step down. Not one."* Walked away from: *"Count's not done!"*

### Home: Greyfall, the Pits, at the Crater's lip

- **first**
  1. Not one step down. Not for you, not for nobody.
  2. Tally. I dug here. Forty seasons, coal and fire for pay.
  3. The last season they never paid. I came back for it.
- **wage** (heard to the end, sets **wageOwed**)
  1. Three hundred and twelve loads. Coal for each. Fire for the long shifts.
  2. It's written. The paymaster wrote everything. Upstairs, in the Counting House.
  3. Get me the page and I'll know I'm not mad. ...Then I'll go.
- **lip**
  1. Men say we dug it. We did. Men say why? Ask who paid.

### Gone, once **warlordDead**

He goes home with nothing; Brenna sells his torch.

### At Greyfall, the Pits, at the Crater's lip, once **ledgerSaved**

- **ledger** (heard to the end the first time, gives *Digger's Torch*)
  1. That's my mark. Three hundred and twelve. Hah! I'm not mad.
  2. And look how deep. They wrote it down. They wrote down how deep.
  3. Here, take the torch. I'm paid. I don't need to see down there any more.
- **paid**
  1. Paid. Late. Paid.

## Master Quill

Greets you: *"Close the door. The draught."* Walked away from: *"The door, please."*

### Home: Greyfall, the High City, the Counting House

- **first**
  1. Close the door. Every draught costs me a page.
  2. Quill. Paymaster to the Citadel. I kept the accounts, and the accounts keep me.
  3. Coal and fire, the goblins were paid in. I pay myself the same way now.
- **stove**
  1. Forty years of ledgers, and a small stove.
  2. I started with the oldest. Fair's fair.
- **cold**
  1. They come to the window sometimes. The taken. Drawn to the light.
  2. They don't knock. They used to queue for their pay just like that.
- **ledger** (once, after meeting, when **wageOwed**; heard to the end, sets **ledgerSaved**)
  1. A goblin wants his page? After all this? ...The last season. I kept it back.
  2. It is the season of the Deep Cut. I could not bring myself to burn that one. Nor to read it.
  3. Take it to him. He was owed. They were all owed.

### At Greyfall, the High City, the Counting House, once **warlordDead**

- **thaw**
  1. The stove's out. I'm warm without it. First time in years.
  2. The last ledger went in on the coldest night. I'm sorry for that one.
  3. All that's left of the Cut is what the goblins remember. And the hole.

## Sister Hush

Greets you: *"Hush."* Walked away from: *"Go warm somewhere else."*

### Home: Greyfall, the Ruined Chapel

- **first**
  1. Hush. You're so loud. All that warmth, shouting.
  2. I am Sister... Sister Hush. Of the Stilled. We pray the fever down.
  3. The Hearth burned too long. The cold is the world getting well.
- **name**
  1. You asked my name, before. I gave you one. I can't think which.
  2. That's the second thing to go. The colour went first. It doesn't hurt.
- **bell** (heard to the end, sets **hushStilled**, gives *Stilled Bell* the first time)
  1. Take the bell. It's for ringing the warm ones quiet. I won't need it.
  2. I'm going down to the Pits. It's coldest there. I'll kneel with the others.
  3. Hush now. ...Hush.

### At Greyfall, the Ruined Chapel, once **warlordDead**

- **fever** (heard to the end the first time, gives *Stilled Bell*)
  1. You killed him, and the fever's coming back. I can feel my hands again.
  2. I was nearly still. Nearly.
  3. Take the bell. I can't ring it for a world that wants to be warm.

### Kneeling in Greyfall, the Pits, once **hushStilled**

Says nothing.
