# Greyfall

The canon for this land. Where it and anything else disagree, this wins; the rules and the world's story are in [../reach.md](../reach.md).

## The land

A crag in the east over a rot marsh, and on it the Citadel, the richest hold
in Aurum because it stood on the richest gold. Its lords broke the Old Law
first and furthest. Their shafts went down year on year, goblins digging and
the horned standing over them, until the last of them, the Deep Cut, broke
into the Hearth. The Crater on the Rotfield is its mouth. The cold came up it
into the Citadel first, and from the Citadel out over the land. Here it still
falls like ash, which is how the land got its name.

The Citadel went grey from the bottom up: the Lower City, the High City, then
the Warlord's Hall. Its garrison is on the walls still, taken at their posts,
the horned among them. The Ruined Chapel is the Stilled's. In the Gallows
Yard the taken keep the folk they carried off from Lanternport's east lane,
walked there because the crag is the warmest place in the east, and it is
warm because of what its lord wears.

On the crown is the Grey Keep, and in it the Grey Warlord, the last lord of
the Citadel, who ordered the Cut and kept himself warm with a crown of the
gold it raised. Behind his throne the Throne Stair goes up to the Throne Isle,
his house's seat at Sunhold's court.

- **Greyfall.** A crag over a rot marsh, and the Citadel on it: the Lower
  City, the High City, the Warlord's Hall and the Grey Keep on its crown. The
  Grey Gate is its front door; the gnomes' Sky Bridge its back.

The Grey took the Citadel mid-shift. Its people kept their posts and lost
the reason for them, so the taken here still do the last work they were
doing: they hoe, hammer, haul and bow, slowly, and never finish. Only the
garrison still fights. The rest do not look up.

- **The Ashen Approach.** The road from the Grey Gate over the marsh, where
  Highward's last sortie died before the walls. The dogs came for the dead
  and stayed for the rest.

- **Rotfield.** The Citadel's fields, under the crag's west face. They fed
  the city once; the rot came down the Cut's spill and soured them. The
  taken farmers still hoe the dead rows and carry nothing in.

- **The Pits.** The Cut's works round the Crater: the headframe, the
  ladders, the spoil heaps, the ore tracks up to the Lower City. Goblins
  dug here, the horned stood over them, and the overseers counted the
  loads. The goblins ran up the ladders when the cold came; the overseers
  and the horned are at their posts still, over empty pits.

- **The Crater.** The Deep Cut's mouth, fallen in on itself after the cold
  came up. Nothing goes down it now, and nothing comes up but the Grey,
  falling back as ash.

- **The Ruined Chapel.** The Order's chapel on the north marsh, the
  Stilled's now. They kneel to the Grey and ring for it.

- **The Lower City.** The crag's skirts: the smelters where the Cut's ore
  was melted, and the goblins' barracks, empty since the night they ran.
  Its taken smiths hammer cold iron and push empty carts up from the Pits.

- **The High City.** The lords' city on the second step, walled. Lords' Row
  runs down its west ward from the Counting House, where the Citadel paid for
  the Cut, keeps its ledgers, and its paymaster burns them. Halfway down the
  row the city's great tree stands dead on the Ash Square. Where the gate
  avenue meets the street under the Hall, the Gilt Market's traders still
  hold out gold gone cold, and east of it the Treasury Rock keeps what gold
  the Citadel had left, guarded yet. The Garrison drills on with the taken
  watch. In the Champions' Pit, sunk into the city with its gallery bridged
  across it, the horned still fight for the lord's guests, who are not
  coming.

- **The Warlord's Hall.** The court round the chasm the Keep stands in. Its
  taken courtiers stand in their rows and bow to the bridge and the empty
  seat past it, because the Warlord sits in the Grey Keep across the chasm
  now, nearer his crown's warmth than theirs.

- **The Gallows Yard.** At the crag's south foot, the pens where the taken
  keep Lanternport's east lane, and the gibbets for the ones who would not
  walk.

## Banner

Ash grey, a crown. The Warlord forgot his name and kept his crown; no one remembers what the Citadel flew. The rule is in [../banners.md](../banners.md).

## Folk

| Tally | Greyfall, the Pits, at the Crater's lip | A goblin digger who came back for the last season's wage the Citadel never paid. Will not go one step down. Wants the ledger that proves it. |
| Master Quill | Greyfall, the High City, the Counting House | The Citadel's paymaster, kept alive by burning its pay ledgers page by page. Gives up the last one to the goblin it owes, if asked in time. |

## For writers only

- The Citadel's pay ledgers are in the High City: who paid the goblins, and
  how deep. Miners' notes in the Lower City say what came up. The Last Ledger,
  the one Quill keeps back, is the season of the Deep Cut itself; Tally
  reads the depth in it and laughs.
- Folk think the Warlord is the Grey. He is not; he is where it drains.
