# The Greyfall

The canon for this land. Where it and anything else disagree, this wins; the rules and the world's story are in [../reach.md](../reach.md).

## The land

- **The Greyfall.** A crag over a rot marsh, and the Citadel on it: the
  Lower City, the High City, the Warlord's Hall and the Grey Keep on its
  crown. The Grey Gate is its front door; the gnomes' Sky Bridge its back.

What the Grey is, and how it took the Citadel's last lord, is told in [../reach.md](../reach.md) under The Grey.
