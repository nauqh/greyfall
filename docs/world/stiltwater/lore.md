# Stiltwater

The canon for this land. Where it and anything else disagree, this wins; the rules and the world's story are in [../reach.md](../reach.md).

## The land

A stilt town on the western shallows: platforms and boardwalks over the
water, the Saltworks, the Fish Market and the Net Lofts, the Eel Pens, and
Lighthouse Rock out on the edge. It has always cut salt and caught fish. It
got rich the winter it learned the Grey will not cross salt water.

Since the Cold Year salt is the dearest thing in Aurum after fire. Every hold
that still trades buys Stiltwater's to ring its doors, and Sunhold buys more
than all of them, in gold, to pack its foundations. Sunhold's coin pays the
militia in the Militia Fort, and the militia wear Sunhold's yellow and keep
the sea road open for its salt. Stiltwater calls that a contract. Lanternport
calls it the one hold that went over to the sky.

Not all of Stiltwater takes the pay. Pirates hold Pirate Cove at the far end
and trade with the finfolk in the channels. Smugglers work the Smugglers'
Steps under the village and sell salt the militia never weighed. Everyone on
the boardwalks grumbles that Sunhold pays in gold that is cooler every year,
and nobody says what that means.

- **Stiltwater.** A stilt town of platforms and boardwalks on the western
  shallows. Its militia wear Sunhold's yellow and take Sunhold's pay to keep
  the sea road; the Grey has not come down to the water. Pirates hold the
  far cove, smugglers the steps under the village, and the Fen Causeway runs
  inland to Stillmere.

## Banner

Salt white, two fish. Salt and fish are its whole trade, and the Grey will not cross salt. The rule is in [../banners.md](../banners.md).

## For writers only

- The cooling gold is Sunhold sinking, slowly. Stiltwater feels it in the
  coin and does not know.
- The finfolk pull the drowned out of the warm water at Tidewake; the
  pirates have heard of it from them. A pirate may hint, never say.
