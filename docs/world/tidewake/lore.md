# Tidewake

The canon for this land. Where it and anything else disagree, this wins; the rules and the world's story are in [../reach.md](../reach.md).

## The land

Under Lanternport the sea runs into the rock, and out of the rock, from
somewhere far inland, runs water that is warm. It is the water that poured
down the sinkhole onto the Hearth, come the long way under Aurum to the sea.
It steams in winter. Fish crowd to it, and Lanternport's fishers kept a boat
in the cave for the catch, until the Cold Year.

Since then the warm water has drawn other things. A few goblins, run from the
Cut and never come as far as Emberwood, live in the cave's dry rooms. A troll
came down to sit where the outflow is warmest and has not moved since, and
the fishers have not had their boat back. And sometimes the water brings in
someone it took, warm and breathing, with no name to give.

- **Tidewake.** The sea cave itself: the grotto where the tide leaves the
  wanderer by the first fire, the goblins' rooms, the castaway's camp and the
  cleft of gold beside it, the flooded gallery, and the antechamber where the
  bones of the ones who went on before lie short of the Ring. The walls are
  scratched with notes by those who woke here before, each where its lesson
  is needed. Lanternport calls them survivors. Nobody in Lanternport has
  counted them.

- **The Bull's Ring.** The round cavern at the cave's end where the warm
  water comes up through the floor, the Cinder Troll sitting in it, and past
  him the sea channel and the fishers' boat. Nobody remembers why it is the
  Bull's Ring; it was named before the troll came.

## For writers only

Never stated in game (see Who knows what in [../reach.md](../reach.md)):

- Hollis and the wanderer are both the drowned, given back warm by the
  Hearth's water. "Another one off the tide" is as near as he comes to it.
- The bones in the antechamber are the drowned who went on and met the
  troll. The notes are the ones who got past him.
- The troll is not guarding anything. It is warm, and the boat is in its way.
