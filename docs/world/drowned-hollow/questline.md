## The Pilgrim

Greets you: *"Softly. It sleeps."* Walked away from: *"Softly, going."*

### Home: Hearthdeep, Pilgrims' Rest (95, 49)

- **first**
  1. Softly... It sleeps on the isle, on the warm. Feel the water.
  2. The Hearth's keeper. The Hearth is under it, and the sisters came down to kneel by it.
  3. We wait for it to wake. ...It will wake.
- **water** (heard to the end, sets **drankClear**; heard to the end the first time, gives *Hollow Water*)
  1. Drink. It is warm. Nothing warm is left up there.
  2. The old one by the fire up there says not to. ...He has never been down.
