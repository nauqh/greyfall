# Brasscrag

The canon for this land. Where it and anything else disagree, this wins; the rules and the world's story are in [../reach.md](../reach.md).

## The land

The gnomes' rock, east of Emberwood and under the sky. Gnomes worked it in
five steps: a town at its foot, a mine on the second step, guns on the third,
the wheel's ropes and gear on the fourth, the Bridgehead on the fifth, and the
Gnome Lift on the Tinker Peak. The Citadel's gold went up that lift to build
Sunhold, and most of the gnomes went with it and became the gnome-kings. The
ones who stayed kept the lift, the Hoist and the Sky Bridge running, and were
paid in the leavings. The name is Lanternport's: brass is what a gnome works
when the gold has all gone up.

Since the Cut no gold goes up and no pay comes down. The bamboo-folk on the
high terraces kept their groves, warm off the gnomes' furnaces, and their
grandsire Old Bamboo walked the wheel that winds the lift, for bamboo and
nothing else. Then the Grey came over the Sky Bridge from Greyfall. Gnolls
took the Bridgehead, the Grey got into Old Bamboo at his wheel, up where the
rock is highest and coldest, and the gnomes fell back down the rock, barring
the Hoist behind them. He still sits on the peak and eats, from habit. His
cubs are warm and stand round him.

The tinkers keep the town and the slingers keep the guns, and the slingers
shoot anything that comes up the steps, because the last thing that did was
grey. Every gnome on the rock can tell you what Sunhold owes them, to the
coin.

- **Brasscrag.** Gnome country: a rock that climbs in five steps to the
  Tinker Peak, cannons on every rim, Old Bamboo on the summit. The lift off
  the peak is the old way up to Sunhold, and the Sky Bridge runs east onto
  Greyfall's wall.

How the rock is laid out, district by district, is in [design.md](design.md).

## For writers only

- The gnomes below never say "gnome-kings" fondly. "Kin" is as warm as it gets.
- Old Bamboo is the one bamboo-folk the Grey has. The rest are not cold.
