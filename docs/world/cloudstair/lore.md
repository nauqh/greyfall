# Cloudstair

The canon for this land. Where it and anything else disagree, this wins; the rules and the world's story are in [../reach.md](../reach.md).

## The land

A chain of drifting isles under Sunhold, each one kept up by a seam of warm
gold the gnomes set in it. They were the stair the gold barges climbed from
the western holds to the gnome-kings, and Highward kept watch posts on them
for a share of the toll: the Watch Isle, the Bell Isle, Windward Rest. The
Watch Lift ran down from the Watch Isle onto Highward's Keep.

The last gold barge came up the year of the Cut and never reached the top. It
lies on the Gold Wreck, and the imps came for the warmth in its cargo and
have not left. With no gold coming up, the isles are cooling, and some ride
lower every year. The watchmen were never relieved; their graves hang off
the isles' undersides. When Highward fell they chained the lift from above so
nothing would climb it, and whoever rides it down unchains it for good.

- **Cloudstair.** Drifting isles that were Highward's watch posts in the
  sky, from the Cloud Bridge down to the Watch Isle and its lift onto the
  Keep. Imps pick over the wrecked gold barge.

## Banner

Sky blue, a watchtower. Highward's watch posts in the sky, their watchmen never relieved. The rule is in [../banners.md](../banners.md).

## For writers only

- An isle sinking a hand's width a year is the plainest proof that Sunhold
  is cooling too. Nobody below has looked up long enough to see it.
