# Brasscrag: the folk

Who lives here, and every line they say. How talks, flags and moving on work is in [../questline.md](../questline.md).

## Pell the Gatekeeper

Greets you: *"Road! Keep to the road!"* Walked away from: *"Road, mind!"*

### Home: Brasscrag, Crag Gate, by the gate tower (12, 48)

- **first**
  1. Road! You're on it. Good. Stay on it.
  2. Pell, of the Crag Gate. Any gnome who comes down off the rock, I let in. Nobody goes up past me.
  3. The slingers up on the rock shoot first. They used to lean over the rim and ask your business, but they've stopped asking.
- **guns**
  1. The last thing to walk up those steps was grey from its cap to its boots. It came up slow and smiling, and the slingers waved it through.
  2. So now they shoot anything that climbs slow, and anything that smiles. Climb quick, and keep a straight face.
- **grove**
  1. North, over the stream, is Old Bamboo's grove. His cubs still sit in it under the stalks, waiting for him to come down.
  2. There are bees in there as well, and bees never sit for anybody.
- **road**
  1. Keep to the road and go east. Tinker Town's at the end of it, and a fire with it.
  2. Still here? Road!
- **died** (once, after meeting, when **died**)
  1. The fire caught you, did it? Better the fire than the guns. The guns don't hand you back.
  2. Road, this time.
- **bamboo** (once, after meeting, when **bambooDead**)
  1. Old Bamboo's dead? Up at his wheel, where he always was?
  2. Hm. I'll go up to the grove and tell the cubs. Someone has to tell the cubs, and it'll not be the slingers.
  3. Road, mind. Road.

## Nim the Tinker

Greets you: *"Mind the fuses."* Walked away from: *"Tick, tick. Off you go."*

Sells: Gnome Powder; Tinker's Buckler.

### Home: Brasscrag, Tinker Town, by the fire (47, 63)

- **first**
  1. Mind the fuses. No, that one. ...Fine. All of them.
  2. Nim, tinker. Powder, springs, brass. Gnome work. Finest in Aurum, and the only.
  3. Gnome work costs gold, at the old rate. The gnome-kings set it themselves, before they went up the lift with all of it.
- **kings**
  1. The kings? Up in Sunhold, that we built for them with our own winches.
  2. They floated off with the gold and left us the rock. Kind of them, wasn't it? Ha.
- **owed**
  1. Sunhold owes this rock a hundred years of pay, and not a grain of gold has come down the lift since the Cut.
  2. I've the sum worked out to the last grain, chalked on a slate by my bench, if you want it. Nobody wants it.
- **ore**
  1. The best ore in Aurum lies under the second step, in Hask's mine.
  2. Nobody mines it now but Hask, and Hask won't climb a foot past it.
- **wheel** (once, after meeting, when **bambooDead**)
  1. The wheel will turn again, then, with Old Bamboo off it. Someone will have to walk it.
  2. Not me. My knees are brass already.

## Moss, the Lift-Keeper's Wife

Greets you: *"Wick? ...No."* Walked away from: *"If you see him..."*

### Home: Brasscrag, Tinker Town, at the Hoist's foot (61, 58)

- **first**
  1. Wick? ...No. You're too tall.
  2. Moss. My Wick kept the lift, up on the wheel step. Old Bamboo walked the wheel for him.
  3. Then one morning the wheel stopped turning, and Old Bamboo never came down off it.
  4. Nor did Wick. Have you seen him up there? A small fellow, with brass buttons all down his coat.
- **hoist**
  1. The Hoist was how we came home of an evening, in a basket straight down the face of the rock.
  2. It's been barred from the top since the gnolls came over the bridge. If you happen up there, lift the bar for me.
  3. Then I'll know the way down is open, if Wick should want it. Please, I beg of you.
- **wait**
  1. I sit here at the Hoist's foot, because it's where he'd come down.
  2. Wick is a careful gnome. He checks every rope twice, and he'd not leave me sitting here without a word. No. What a horrible thought.
- **hoisted** (once, after meeting, when **hoistOpen**)
  1. You lifted the bar. I heard it go, all the way down here.
  2. ...Only you came down it, though.

### At Brasscrag, Tinker Peak, by the fire under the lift (74, 17), once **bambooDead**

- **coat**
  1. Wick's coat was on the wheel, brass buttons and all, and no Wick in it. He'd never have let Old Bamboo walk alone.
  2. Someone has to keep the lift, and I watched him do it twenty winters. I'll keep it till he comes back for his coat.
- **up**
  1. Step in, if you're going up.
  2. The kings won't thank you. They never thanked him.

## Pip

Greets you: *"Are you going up?"* Walked away from: *"Go up! Go up!"*

### Home: Brasscrag, Tinker Town, at the Hoist's foot (63, 59)

- **first**
  1. Are you going up? Grown folk go up and don't come down.
  2. I'm Pip. My da keeps the lift. Kept. ...Keeps.
  3. Bamboo used to let me ride on his back while he walked the wheel. Round and round and round.
- **slingers**
  1. The slingers shot at me once. On purpose, I think.
  2. I wasn't slow. I wasn't smiling either.

### At Brasscrag, Tinker Peak (79, 16), once **bambooDead**

- **sick**
  1. Mum says Bamboo was sick. The grey kind.
  2. She says you made him stop hurting. ...That's what she says.
- **lift**
  1. I'm going to keep the lift when I'm big.
  2. Round and round. Someone has to.

## Hask the Miner

Greets you: *"Not going up."* Walked away from: *"Not going up. Said so."*

### Home: Brasscrag, the Works, by the mine (69, 50)

- **first**
  1. Not going up. Don't ask.
  2. Hask. I dig the second step. The second. Not the third.
  3. The third step's the guns, and the slingers up there don't know my face from a grey one's any more.
- **gold**
  1. There's gold in the mine behind me, still in the walls. Take what you can prise out. Up's not worth it.
  2. Down's not worth it either. Hah. The second step is as far as I go, either way.
- **quiet** (once, after meeting, when **bambooDead**)
  1. The guns went quiet a whole morning. First time in three winters.
  2. ...I still won't go up. But I looked.

## Master Fennick

Greets you: *"Wheel first."* Walked away from: *"Cage second..."*

### Home: Brasscrag, the Workshop (57, 13)

- **first**
  1. Wheel first, cage second, gnome last. Say it.
  2. No? Nobody says it any more. They put the gold first and the gnome nowhere, and look at the rock now.
  3. Master Fennick, builder of the lift. I may be but small, but I built the kings a road to the sky. They rode it up, and never sent it down.
- **cage**
  1. That cage? A new one, oiled and hung, for when the wheel turns again.
  2. It won't turn while Old Bamboo sits in it, grey to the eyes and chewing as if nothing had happened.
- **gantry**
  1. The gnolls don't cross my gantry. It's too narrow for anything bigger than a gnome, and they've tried.
  2. I built it narrow on purpose. Ha. Gnome last, but not this once.
- **cog** (once, after meeting, when **bambooDead**; heard to the end the first time, gives *Wheel Cog*)
  1. You put Old Bamboo down, then. Good. He'd have hated what he'd become.
  2. This came off the wheel when it stopped. His feet wore it bright.
  3. Take it. The new cage can have a new cog. Wheel first, cage second.

## Gill the Fisher

Greets you: *"Fog's coming."* Walked away from: *"Watch the bridge."*

### Home: Brasscrag, Windward Shore (102, 53)

- **first**
  1. Fog's coming. Fog always comes off Greyfall.
  2. Gill, fisher. I fish under the bridge. The fish don't care what crosses over.
  3. I do, though. I've learned to look up between bites.
- **fog**
  1. They came over the bridge in the fog, dogs on two legs running with the grey ones, and laughing as they ran.
  2. I counted till I couldn't. Then I hid.
- **bridge**
  1. The Sky Bridge runs straight across to the Citadel's wall, and there's no gate on it anywhere.
  2. There's only the dogs at this end to stop you. Mind the dogs.
- **guns** (once, after meeting, when **bambooDead**)
  1. There are gnomes on the peak again. I can see their caps from here.
  2. Lighting the guns, by the look. Good. Light them.
