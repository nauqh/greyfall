# Brasscrag: the folk

Who lives here, and every line they say. How talks, flags and moving on work is in [../questline.md](../questline.md).

## Nim the Tinker

Greets you: *"Mind the fuses."* Walked away from: *"Tick, tick. Off you go."*

Sells: Gnome Powder; Tinker's Buckler.

### Home: Brasscrag, Tinker Town, by the fire (47, 63)

- **first**
  1. Mind the fuses. No, that one. ...Fine. All of them.
  2. Nim, tinker. Powder, springs, brass. Gnome work. Finest in Aurum, and the only.
  3. Gnome work costs gold, at the old rate. The gnome-kings set it themselves, before they went up the lift with all of it.

## Moss, the Lift-Keeper's Wife

Greets you: *"Wick? ...No."* Walked away from: *"If you see him..."*

### Home: Brasscrag, Tinker Town, at the Hoist's foot (61, 58)

- **first**
  1. Wick? ...No. You're too tall.
  2. Moss. My Wick kept the lift, up on the wheel step. Old Bamboo walked the wheel for him.
  3. Then one morning the wheel stopped turning, and Old Bamboo never came down off it.
  4. Nor did Wick. Have you seen him up there? A small fellow, with brass buttons all down his coat.
- **hoist**
  1. The Hoist was how we came home of an evening, in a basket straight down the face of the rock.
  2. It's been barred from the top since the gnolls came over the bridge. If you happen up there, lift the bar for me.
  3. Then I'll know the way down is open, if Wick should want it. Please, I beg of you.
- **wait**
  1. I sit here at the Hoist's foot, because it's where he'd come down.
  2. Wick is a careful gnome. He checks every rope twice, and he'd not leave me sitting here without a word. No. What a horrible thought.
- **hoisted** (once, after meeting, when **hoistOpen**)
  1. You lifted the bar. I heard it go, all the way down here.
  2. ...Only you came down it, though.

### At Brasscrag, Tinker Peak, by the fire under the lift (74, 17), once **bambooDead**

- **coat**
  1. Wick's coat was on the wheel, brass buttons and all, and no Wick in it. He'd never have let Old Bamboo walk alone.
  2. Someone has to keep the lift, and I watched him do it twenty winters. I'll keep it till he comes back for his coat.
- **cog** (heard to the end the first time, gives *Wheel Cog*)
  1. This was in his coat pocket, a cog off the wheel. Old Bamboo's feet wore it bright.
  2. Wick always kept a spare. Take it. The lift turns on the new one now.
- **up**
  1. Step in, if you're going up.
  2. The kings won't thank you. They never thanked him.
