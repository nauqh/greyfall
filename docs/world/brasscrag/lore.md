# Brasscrag

The canon for this land. Where it and anything else disagree, this wins; the rules and the world's story are in [../reach.md](../reach.md).

## The land

The gnomes' rock, east of Emberwood and under the sky. Gnomes worked it in
five steps: a town at its foot, a mine on the second step, guns on the third,
the wheel's ropes and gear on the fourth, the Bridgehead on the fifth, and the
Gnome Lift on the Tinker Peak. The Citadel's gold went up that lift to build
Sunhold, and most of the gnomes went with it and became the gnome-kings. The
ones who stayed kept the lift, the Hoist and the Sky Bridge running, and were
paid in the leavings. The name is Lanternport's: brass is what a gnome works
when the gold has all gone up.

Since the Cut no gold goes up and no pay comes down. The bamboo-folk on the
high terraces kept their groves, warm off the gnomes' furnaces, and their
grandsire Old Bamboo walked the wheel that winds the lift, for bamboo and
nothing else. Then the Grey came over the Sky Bridge from Greyfall. Gnolls
took the Bridgehead, the Grey got into Old Bamboo at his wheel, up where the
rock is highest and coldest, and the gnomes fell back down the rock, barring
the Hoist behind them. He still sits on the peak and eats, from habit. His
cubs are warm and stand round him.

The tinkers keep the town and the slingers keep the guns, and the slingers
shoot anything that comes up the steps, because the last thing that did was
grey. Every gnome on the rock can tell you what Sunhold owes them, to the
coin.

- **Brasscrag.** Gnome country: a rock that climbs in five steps to the
  Tinker Peak, cannons on every rim, Old Bamboo on the summit. The lift off
  the peak is the old way up to Sunhold, and the Sky Bridge runs east onto
  Greyfall's wall.

Nobody on Brasscrag was taken but Old Bamboo. Every place on the rock is
still worked, by gnomes who know the work goes nowhere now: the ore has no
lift to go up, the guns have nothing to shoot but the steps, and the pay is
a sum they say aloud.

- **Crag Gate.** Where the Crag Stair comes up out of Emberwood's smoke.
  The gnomes kept a toll here for the goods going up; now they keep a watch
  for anything coming up, and send it along the road with a warning.

- **Bamboo Grove.** Old Bamboo's own ground, under the rock's west face,
  where the bamboo-folk cut cane and sold it to the gnomes for gantry
  poles. His cubs sit in the thicket with the bones of the gnomes the bees
  caught, and the West Shoulder's two guns face away from it.

- **Tinker Town.** The rock's foot, where the gnomes who did not go up
  live. It mended the lift's gear and made the cages. Its tinkers still
  make gear for a lift that does not run, and its yards are full of it.

- **The Terraces.** The gnomes' fields on the southern spur, and the Sea
  Watch at its end, which watched for the ships that brought grain for
  gold. The farmers still feed the town; the Sea Watch watches the bridge.

- **The Works.** The second step and its mine. The gold the lift carried
  came out of here. The miners still dig and stack it in the ore yard,
  because no gnome on the rock will be the one to stop.

- **The Batteries.** The third step: the gun line, raised to keep the
  rock's gold from anyone who came up for it. The slingers still man it,
  and now shoot anything on the steps. The Hoist, which carried their shot
  up from the Works, is barred from this side.

- **The Wheel Step.** The fourth step, where the lift's ropes ran up to
  the wheel. The ropes hang slack, and the wheel timbers lie where the
  gnomes cut them loose.

- **The Workshop.** The master tinker's yard on its own knob, where the
  lift cages were built. A half-built cage stands in it still.

- **The Bridgehead.** The fifth step, where the Sky Bridge leaves for
  Greyfall's wall and the summit path climbs to the Tinker Peak. The gnomes
  kept a toll on the bridge; the gnolls came over it in fog and hold its
  gatehouse.

- **The East Ridge.** The rock's back way up from the shore, and the watch
  over Greyfall. The slingers walk it in the wind.

- **Windward Shore.** The fishers' strand under the ridge. One gnome
  still fishes it.

How the rock is laid out, district by district, is in [design.md](design.md).

## For writers only

- The gnomes below never say "gnome-kings" fondly. "Kin" is as warm as it gets.
- Old Bamboo is the one bamboo-folk the Grey has. The rest are not cold.
