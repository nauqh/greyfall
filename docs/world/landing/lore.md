# The Landing

The canon for this land. Where it and anything else disagree, this wins; the rules and the world's story are in [../reach.md](../reach.md).

## The land

- **The Landing and the Bull's Ring.** The sea cave under the Harbour where
  the tide leaves the wanderer, with notes scratched by those who woke there
  before, and the Cinder Troll keeping the fishers' boat out.
