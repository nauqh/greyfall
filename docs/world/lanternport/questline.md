## Old Ferrow

Greets you: *"Ah. Another one."* Walked away from: *"Mind the dark, then."*

### Home: Lanternport, the Lower Town, by the bonfire (51, 56)

- **first**
  1. ...Hm. Another one the tide spat out.
  2. Name's Ferrow. I keep Lanternport's fire. Someone has to.
  3. The last one sat right where you're standing. Warmed his hands, then went east.
  4. He didn't come back. They never do. Hah...
- **shore**
  1. You'll want the lanterns. They run straight up from the fire, to the Burg Gate on the crown.
  2. Someone lit them before you came. ...Someone always does.
  3. There's a path up through the graves to the Moor Gate, too. Barred from this side.
  4. Not that the dogs on that hill care much for bars.
- **bull** (once, after meeting, when **bullWon**)
  1. So the tide brought you back. That troll in the Ring, I'd wager.
  2. He'll keep. Take the boat back when your arm is stronger.
  3. Whatever he guards is worth a fall or two. Hm.
- **goal**
  1. You'll be wanting the Grey Gate, east past the moor. Everyone does, in the end.
  2. Two locks on it. Kingsrest buried one key, with the king under the barrows.
  3. The other we threw down our own Old Well. Landed in the Weaver's web, they say, and further down still.
- **castle**
  1. The Keep? Shut, since the Burg turned. Its steward ran for the wood with the Ember Key.
  2. A bull had him for supper, so they say... Still has the key, I'd wager.
- **well**
  1. The Old Well only goes one way. Down.
  2. And don't drink what's at the bottom. Water that cold has the Grey in it.
- **roads**
  1. Every road out of here comes back to this fire. The gates, the well, the lift.
  2. Folk find that a comfort. ...I've never been sure.
- **rest**
  1. Rest, if you must. The roads are patient as stones.
- **died** (once, after meeting, when **died**)
  1. Back so soon? Don't look at me like that.
  2. Everyone comes back. That's the trouble with this place. Hah...
- **key** (once, after meeting, when **trollKey**)
  1. That key's warm. Bull's blood, or the fire inside the brute.
  2. Either way, keep it away from my kindling.
- **warlord** (once, after meeting, when **warlordDead**)
  1. So the Warlord's dead, they're saying. Hm.
  2. Felt warmer this morning. Not warm, mind. I'll keep the fire going anyway. Habit.
- **ansel** (once, after meeting, when **anselRoused**)
  1. Ansel's off his wall. I can see it from here. First time in years.
  2. They come back, or they don't. The wall will keep.
- **east** (once, after meeting, when **greyfallReached**)
  1. You went east... and you came back.
  2. That's one more than the last one managed.
- **gone** (once, after meeting, when **anselAhead**)
  1. Ansel's not come back off the moor road. Nor will he, I'd wager.
  2. They go east. ...They always go east.

## Wren

- **first**
  1. You walk loud, you know. That's fine. An archer likes a loud friend up front.
  2. I'm Wren. I keep the butts, and the bows.
  3. Put gold on the barrel, and I'll send you someone who doesn't miss. ...Much.
- **goblins**
  1. Goblins at the Ember Gate camp in threes.
  2. Shoot the torch first. Spears are slow. Torches are rude.
- **back**

  2. If you can smell it, you're too close.
- **wings** (once, after meeting, when **died**)

## Garth the Woodcutter

Greets you: *"Mind the axe."* Walked away from: *"Go on, then."*

### Home: Lanternport (27, 44)

- **first**
  1. Hm? You're not from the wood. Nobody's from the wood any more.
  2. Garth. I cut hardwood at the Palisade, past the Ember Gate. Before.
  3. Now I cut what grows this side. Poor stuff. Hah...
- **gate**
  1. The goblins came over our fence one night. We ran for the gate.
  2. Dunn had it barred before the last of us were through. ...Can't say I wouldn't have.
- **bar**
  1. The bar only lifts from the wood's side now.
  2. Someone would have to go round, through Highward. ...Not me.

### At Emberwood, the Palisade (44, 64), once **emberGateOpen**

- **back**
  1. You opened it. From the wrong side, too. Hah...
  2. So we're back at the Palisade. Someone has to cut what the goblins haven't burnt.
- **note**
  1. Found a note in the camp. "They barred it with us still outside."
  2. A fine hand. A Keep hand. ...It was the steward knocking, that night.
  3. He went north, after. Nobody comes back from north.
- **trunks**
  1. The ground's warm under these trunks. The heat comes up into them.
  2. They burn slow, and they burn twice.
- **bull** (once, after meeting, when **trollKey**)
  1. That bull in the hollow. Came out of the east the winter the cold came, and lay down in the ash.
  2. The goblins feed it like a lord. Bowed to it, some of them. I saw.
  3. Hah. Don't tell me how it looked at the end. I'd sooner not know.

## Dunn of the Gate Watch

  2. There was knocking, after. A long while. I didn't open.
- **barred**

- **watch**

  2. Woodcutters cut. I watch. The goblins come in threes, same as before.
- **steward**
  1. Garth showed me the note. The steward, knocking at my gate, with the Keep's key on him.
  2. If I'd opened, that key would be in the Keep. Not up the Ash Road.
  3. ...Go on. Say it.
- **trees**

- **bull** (once, after meeting, when **trollKey**)

  2. So the steward got past my gate, and the goblins, and the heat. And their beast had him at the end of it.
  3. ...I barred the gate on him. Hm.

## Tobin

Greets you: *"Don't lean on the rope."* Walked away from: *"...Right. Off you go."*

### Home: Lanternport, the Lower Town, by the Old Well (44, 53)

- **first**
  1. Don't lean on that rope. It's older than me.
  2. That's the Old Well, in the hill there. Not for drawing water.
  3. My brother went down it after a dropped coin. ...Never came up.
  4. Says something about the coin, doesn't it?
- **rule**
  1. No climbing back up. That's the rule of the well.
  2. Funny, a hole having rules.
- **wings**
  1. Something else lives down there. Wings. You hear them before you see them.
  2. A sword's no use on a thing that won't come down to meet it.
  3. Wren hires out bows, at the butts by the west road. Take one, if you go.
- **chewed** (once, after meeting, when **died**)
  1. You look chewed on.
  2. If it had wings, your steel won't reach it. Wren's bows will.
- **below** (once, after meeting, when **wellDescended**; heard to the end, sets **tobinDown**)
  1. You came back? From down there?
  2. ...Did you see him? Thin fellow. Quick hands. Always smiling.
  3. No. Course not. ...Forget I asked.

### At Underwell, Smugglers' Nook (11, 9), once **tobinDown**

- **nook**
  1. Don't look so surprised. There's no rule against going down, is there?
  2. Smugglers keep this corner. One of them is wearing his coat.
  3. Says he swapped it, deeper in. Ha. My brother never swapped a thing in his life.
- **deeper** (heard to the end, sets **tobinDeeper**)
  1. I'm going on, through the cisterns.
  2. Don't wait for me. ...Nobody ever has.

### At Graveroad, Robbers' Camp (7, 69), once **tobinDeeper**

- **camp**
  1. Found him. He runs this camp. Thin as ever. Smiling.
  2. He says the coin was never dropped. He threw it, to see who'd come after.
  3. ...I came after.
- **stay** (heard to the end the first time, gives *The Thrown Coin*)
  1. I'm staying. Someone has to count what he steals.
  2. Here. The coin. I found it at the bottom, the day I came down. He never looked for it.
  3. Quick hands run in the family, turns out.

## Mother Ysolde

Greets you: *"Peace on you, child."* Walked away from: *"Go gently."*

### Home: Lanternport, The Upper Town (43, 24)

- **first**
  1. Peace on you, child. I am Ysolde, a Keeper of the Order, and mother to it here.
  2. My sisters went down to the Drowned Chapel. The warmth was below us, they said, and they would kneel by it.
  3. I keep the fire up here. ...They did not come back.
- **stair** (heard to the end, sets **sistersStair**)
  1. Beside the chapel there is a stair. The sisters climbed it to the Ward to pray, before the Grey.
  2. I've no knees for it now. The bar lifts from this side.
  3. Go up, if you've the stomach for what walks the cloister.
- **candle**
  1. I light a candle each night.
  2. The hour comes round, and my hands are already there.
- **clean** (once, after meeting, when **drankClear**)
  1. You drank the warm water, child. I can feel it on you, like a hearth through a wall.
  2. ...Nothing that warm should lie so far down. Remember that.

  4. One is a mercy. I'll not ask the dark for more.
- **lake** (heard to the end, sets **ysoldeDeeper**)
  1. She speaks in her sleep. Of water under all of this, warm as a hand, and gold shining in the rock.
  2. The others went on to it. I must go too.
  3. Do not follow. ...Or do. I am too old to forbid things.
- **clean** (once, after meeting, when **drankClear**)
  1. You drank the warm water, child. I can feel it on you, like a hearth through a wall.

## Maren the Harbourmaster

Greets you: *"Tide's out."* Walked away from: *"Fair winds. Ha."*

### Home: Lanternport, the Docks, at the boat's pier (67, 63)

- **first** (heard to the end, she walks you to the square's fire and has you light it, if it is cold)
  1. Tide's out. ...Maren. I keep the harbour, for all the good it does.
  2. No ships in three winters.
  3. Stiltwater still trades down the coast, if you can stomach its prices. And its pirates.
  4. Mostly the pirates. Ha.
  5. Square fire's gone cold again. Ferrow dozes. ...Come on, I'll show you.
- **finfolk**
  1. Finfolk off the Stiltwater shore. Sharks, the pirates call them. Don't swim there.
  2. They pull things out of the warm water by the caves. Breathing things, the pirates say. ...I don't ask.
- **lantern**
  1. The lantern on the wall? I light it every night. For ships.
  2. Three winters, no ships. You don't stop lighting it. That's how you lose it.
- **tide**
  1. Tide's out. It's always out, lately.
  2. As if the sea's leaving too.
- **sail** (once, after meeting, when **warlordDead**; heard to the end, sets **marenSails**)
  1. A sail. Off the point, this morning. First in three winters.
  2. Stiltwater, likely. Pirates, likelier. I don't care.
  3. Thirty years I've watched that water. I'd like to be on it once, before it leaves for good.

### Gone, once **marenSails**

## Dunstan the Foreman

Greets you: *"Mind the carts."* Walked away from: *"Back to it."*

### Home: Lanternport, the Mine Hills, by the mine (96, 25)

- **first**
  1. Mind the carts. ...Dunstan. I run the Mine Hills, what's left to run.
  2. The good ore came off the deep face, under the crest. Forty loads a week.
  3. Then one shift it came up grey. Cold in the hand. Cold in high summer.
  4. The lads who worked it went slow. Slower every day. So I nailed it shut myself.
- **face**
  1. We work the old face now. Soft stuff. Brenna grumbles.
  2. Let her grumble. She's still got all her lads.
- **boards**
  1. Don't touch the boards.
  2. I count them every morning. Nineteen. ...Still nineteen.
- **warm** (once, after meeting, when **warlordDead**)
  1. Put my hand to the boards this morning. Warm. First time in three winters.
  2. Maybe I'll pull them. ...Maybe next year.
