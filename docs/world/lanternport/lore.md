# Lanternport

The canon for this land. Where it and anything else disagree, this wins; the rules and the world's story are in [../reach.md](../reach.md).

## The land

Below Highward, Lanternport, the town, alone held. It held because it is on
the salt, which the Grey has never crossed; because Highward stood between it
and the east for as long as Highward stood; and because Old Ferrow never let
its fire go out. It lost its east lane in the first nights; the taken carried
its people off to Greyfall's Gallows Yard, walking them toward the warmest
place in the east. Then its people barred the gates, kept the harbour, and
kept the fire. No ship has put in for three winters.

The town was named for the lantern on its harbour wall, lit every night for
ships. It is still lit. Nobody says why.

- **Lanternport.** The hub: a town in a bowl round the one bonfire, with
  the Old Well in the hill's cave west of it, the docks below and Highward
  above. It held.

- **The Mine Hills.** The town's worked hill, north-east of the town,
  its quarried face stepping up to a crest over the sea. The best ore came
  off the deep face under the crest until, one shift in the first winter, it
  came up grey: cold in the hand, and the men who worked it went slow. The
  foreman nailed the cave shut, and the hill works only the old face now,
  which is why its ore is soft. The deep face had gone further down than a
  grave; the Kingsrest folk who still come to market say that is all it
  took. Lanternport does not like the thought, and the Underwell under the
  Old Well is why.

- **The Weighhouse and the Lane House.** The east ring's two halls. Every
  load off the Mine Hills is weighed and paid out at the Weighhouse, on the
  back street at the hill's foot, before it goes up the Highward road to the
  forge; since the deep face was shut, half its scales stand idle. The Lane
  House, at the head of the ring's terrace under the east lane's tower, was
  the east lane's guild hall. The lane went in the first nights, its people carried
  off to the Gallows Yard; the few who got out sleep in the hall now, and
  the town keeps a lamp in its window for the rest.

## Folk

| Who | Where | Their place in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Old Ferrow | Lanternport, by the fire | Kept the watch when the town held; keeps the fire now. Sends the wanderer on and says why the Keep is shut. |
| Tobin | Lanternport, by the Old Well | His brother went down the well after a coin. Follows him down to Graveroad and stays to count what he steals. |
| Mother Ysolde | Lanternport, the Upper Town | A Keeper of the Order. Her sisters went the Pilgrims' way, down to the Drowned Chapel; she follows the one freed down to Hearthdeep, where the rest kneel drowned by the warm water. |
| Wren | Lanternport, the butts in West Fields | Hires out Lanternport's bows. Goes shooting goblins in Emberwood once the Ember Bull is dead. |
| Dunstan the Foreman | Lanternport, the Mine Hills | Boarded the deep face when its ore came up grey, and counts the boards every morning. Feels them warm once the Warlord falls, and still leaves them up. |
| Maren the Harbourmaster | Lanternport, the docks | Has watched an empty sea for three winters. Sails on the first ship after the Warlord falls. |
| The Moor Watchman | Lanternport, the Moor Watch | Keeps the Moor Gate onto Kingsrest barred on orders. Once it is opened from the moor, goes to look at the lights, and into the barrows. |
| Penny | Lanternport, hiding in West Fields | Escaped when the east lane was taken. Follows the wanderer to the Gallows Yard to be with her mum; freed when the Warlord falls. |
