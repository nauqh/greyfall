# Graveroad

The canon for this land. Where it and anything else disagree, this wins; the rules and the world's story are in [../reach.md](../reach.md).

## The land

Under Kingsrest the old kings were carried down their grave-roads to lie as
deep as the Old Law allowed, and not a step deeper. The roads stop at the
line: the Ossuary Galleries, the Great Barrow, the King's Stair down from the
Barrow King's hall. Below the line is the dark the law was kept above, older
caves cut by water, and the roads now run on into them, because things
broke through from both sides. The Dig came in from Lanternport. The Collapsed
Hall fell into the caves under it. The Drowned Graves filled with water
seeping down from Stillmere toward the Hearth.

Tobin's brother went down the Old Well after a coin and found the kings'
gold, which is warm, and set up the Robbers' Camp to dig it out. In the
deepest cave the Weaver nests, a spider grown old and large on the warmth
coming up from below, and in her web hangs the key Lanternport threw down the
well. The Sinking Stair goes down past her, deeper still, toward the Hearth.

- **Graveroad.** The old kings' grave-roads under the moor, and the caves
  past the end of them, down to the Weaver's Web. The Weaver keeps the Silk
  Key. Tobin's brother runs the robbers' camp.
