# Underwell

The canon for this land. Where it and anything else disagree, this wins; the rules and the world's story are in [../reach.md](../reach.md).

## The land

Lanternport's cisterns and catacombs under the Old Well: the Well Bottom, the
Cisterns and the Cistern Run, the Ossuary, black water and spiders in the
pillars. The town has drawn its water here and buried its dead here since
before Highward was built.

The Drowned Chapel is the Order's, half under the cistern water. Ysolde's
sisters went down to it the Pilgrims' way, to pray on their way to the Hearth,
and did not come back up.

The Dig is Lanternport's own. After the Cold Year the town dug east from the
cisterns for warm gold to keep its fire and its doors, under the moor and
deeper than a grave, until it broke into the old kings' grave-roads. It stopped digging the same winter Dunstan boarded the
Mine Hills' deep face, and has not spoken of either since. It is the Old Law
broken again, small, by the one hold that held. Smugglers use the tunnel now,
from their nook.

When the Grey Gate was barred, Lanternport threw its key, the Silk Key,
down the Old Well, so nobody could open the gate. The well goes further down than the town knew.

- **Underwell.** Lanternport's cisterns and catacombs under the Old Well:
  black water, spiders in the pillars, smugglers in their nook, the Drowned
  Chapel where Ysolde's sisters went down, and the Dig nobody admits to.

## Banner

Cistern umber, a well. The cisterns and catacombs under the Old Well, where the Silk Key went down. The rule is in [../banners.md](../banners.md).
