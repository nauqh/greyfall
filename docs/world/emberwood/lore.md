# Emberwood

The canon for this land. Where it and anything else disagree, this wins; the rules and the world's story are in [../reach.md](../reach.md).

## The land

West of Highward the Hearth runs shallow. The ground is warm underfoot, the
springs come up hot, and the Ember Run steams the length of the wood. Over it
grew a jungle no northern land should have: broad leaves, vines, flowers red
as coals, and air that is never cold. Only on the cooler western edge do the
old pines still stand. Highward cut its hardwood here and floated it down to
Lanternport through the Ember Gate.

Since the Cold Year it is the warmest place left on the surface, and
everything that fears the cold has crowded into it. The goblins came first,
up the Cut with their torches lit and west across the land to the one place
their fires were not needed and they lit them anyway. They burned clearings
for their camps and took the trees above them for their warrens. Gnolls sit
on Smoke Bluff, on the wood's edge, where the Grey eats at the leaves and
never gets in far. The lumber camp ran for the gate; Lanternport barred it
behind them.

The Ember Hollow, at the head of the Ash Road, is the hottest ground in the
wood: a pan of ash over the vents, where the earth smokes. The goblins keep
a lord there. He is one of the horned, the Citadel's oathsworn guard, who
watched over its pits while goblins dug them. When the cold came up the Cut
every bullman stayed at his post and went grey on it. This one did not. He
walked west after the goblins to the warmest ground he could find and lay
down in it, and the hot ash took to his hide and stayed. The goblins still
do what a horn tells them; they did it for a hundred years in the shafts.
They feed him, wall him in, and call him the Ember Bull.

The steward of Highward came up the Ash Road with the Ember Key, looking for
the way round the goblins' ford, and met him. The key hangs on the Bull's
harness now. A bullman's oath is to a house, and he has none left. Whose key
he thinks he is keeping is not said.

- **Emberwood.** A jungle over warm ground, the old pines on its west edge.
  Goblin warrens up in the trees, gnolls on Smoke Bluff, and the Ember Bull
  in the Ember Hollow with the steward's key.

Every place in the wood was Highward's work or is the goblins' now. What
Highward left, the goblins took and use for something else: the lumber
camp is a war camp's outpost, the timber road feeds a bull, and only the
pines on the cold edge are left as they were.

- **Postern Crag.** Highward's back door into the wood, a stair off the
  Belfry down three ledges. The steward came down it with the Keep's key
  and his escort. The escort lies on the second ledge; the goblins keep a
  spear post on the first, and nothing has come down since.

- **The Burn.** The jungle the goblins burned for their war camp. They
  live round one great fire they never let go out, and bring what they
  take back to the Chief's Mound. Every warband in the wood goes out from
  here and comes back here.

- **The Palisade.** Highward's lumber camp at the Ember Gate, where the
  hardwood was cut and floated down to Lanternport. The woodcutters ran for
  the gate and the town barred it behind them; their saws and stacks lie
  where they dropped them, and the goblins sleep in the woodcutters' huts.

- **Old Pines.** The cool west edge, the one stand nobody burned. The
  goblins will not live where it is cold, so the beasts do. A hermit of the
  Order keeps the pool.

- **Smoke Bluff.** The gnolls' rock over the Run, at the wood's edge where
  the Grey eats at the leaves. The gnolls sit on it and shoot down on the
  Pine Ford road. The goblins leave them be.

- **Warren Hill.** The goblins' home, huts stacked on a hill in two
  heights round the watch keep Highward built on its crest to guard the
  timber road. The goblins took the keep with the hill and kept its alarm
  bell; they answer it as they answered a horn in the shafts, and when it
  rings they clear the ford for whatever comes. Their young, their larders
  and their hoard are up on it; the warband comes back to it to sleep. The
  Treetop Walk runs from it over the Run, barred at the hill's end. Behind
  it the old cutting runs along the coast, where a woodcutter hid and did
  not come out.

- **Cinder Ford.** Where Highward's timber road crossed the Run, and the
  carts waited for the water to fall. The goblins hold the south bank and
  take a toll of whatever crosses, in meat.

- **The Ash Road.** The timber road's north end, which ran up to the
  hollow's cutting. The goblins line it with skulls and carry the Bull's
  meat up it every day. The steward walked it and did not come back.

- **Crag Foot.** The gnomes' prospect under Brasscrag, where they dug the
  wood's gold out of the rock and sent it up the Crag Stair. Cut off from
  their kin, the prospectors still dig and stack, and keep a goblin post in
  sight of their door.

How the wood is laid out, district by district, is in [design.md](design.md).

## Banner

Ember red, horns. Flowers red as coals, and the Ember Bull in the hottest ground. The rule is in [../banners.md](../banners.md).

## For writers only

- Lanternport and the folk think the Ember Bull is a beast the goblins keep.
  Nobody below says "bullman" of him; Brasscrag's gnomes might.
- He is not taken. He is the one horned who broke his post for warmth, and
  the only one the Grey never had. What the horned in Greyfall would make of
  him is a question for later.
