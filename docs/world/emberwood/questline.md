## Brother Fenn

Greets you: *"Softly. The pines listen."* Walked away from: *"Go softly, Tidewashed."*

### Home: Emberwood, Old Pines, by the hermit's hut (23, 64)

- **first**
  1. Softly, softly. You'll wake the bears. ...Or they'll wake you.
  2. I am Fenn, of the Order once, and of these pines now. The pines ask less of me.
  3. The goblins burn everything but here. They will not come under these trees. I have never asked why.
- **plank**
  1. The goblins drew their plank up off the tree walk. Stand on the bluff all you like, it will not come down.
  2. Go round. North over the stones, behind their hill. Then drop it from their side.
- **thicket**
  1. The bears keep something in the thicket behind them. Gold, by the shine.
  2. Bears do not spend it. ...Nor do I.
- **hearth**
  1. Put your hand to the ground, past the pines. Warm, is it not? The Order calls it the Hearth, the warmth under everything.
  2. Here it rises near the top. That is why the whole wood crowds in. ...Cold things know where warm is.
- **pray**
  1. I pray for the pines. Someone should.
  2. Go softly. May the Hearth keep you.
- **cooling** (once, after meeting, when **trollKey**)
  1. The bull is dead? Then the goblins will spill out of the hollow, seeking a new lord.
  2. Or a new fire. ...Pray it is not these pines.

## Tam the Prospector

Greets you: *"Oi! Off my seam."* Walked away from: *"Mind the edge."*

### Home: Emberwood, Crag Foot, by the gnomes' hut (93, 15)

- **first**
  1. Oi! That's my seam you're on. ...No, go on. Nobody else is coming up for it.
  2. Tam, prospector. Came up from the Mine Hills after better ore. The goblins shut the road behind me.
  3. The gnomes let me dig, so long as I don't dig near them. Fair trade, I say. Ha.
- **bridge**
  1. Listen, this might be of use. There's a bridge under the rock to the east, and it takes you round the goblins' camp to the ford.
  2. There's a fire at the ford, the last warm thing you'll find before the bull.
- **seams**
  1. Gold shows in the rock up here, if you've the eye. Warm in the hand, when it's good.
  2. Take what you can carry. I can't carry much. Never could.
- **home** (once, after meeting, when **trollKey**)
  1. Bull's dead? Then the road's open, and I can go home.
  2. ...The gold's still in the rock, mind you. I'll go home next year. Ha.
