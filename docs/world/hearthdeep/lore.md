# Hearthdeep

The canon for this land. Where it and anything else disagree, this wins; the rules and the world's story are in [../reach.md](../reach.md).

## The land

The bottom of the world, under Stillmere: the hollow the Deep Cut broke into.
It was warm and gold and dry. Then the fen poured down the sinkhole onto it
for a season, and now it is a still, clear lake with the Hearth burning under
the water, gold glowing in the rock round it. The water is warm. It runs out
under Aurum to the sea and comes up at Tidewake.

The trees that grew in the warmth stand in the shallows as stone: the Stone
Grove, the end of the Grey, where the cold of the quenching first took them. At Pilgrims' Rest the Order's Pilgrims kneel by the warm water, and wait
for what sleeps on the Shell Isle to wake. Some have knelt so long they will
not get up.

The Shell Below is a beast, a turtle that came down for the warmth long ago
and has grown on it for longer than Aurum has had kings. It sleeps on the
isle, over the Hearth's warmest water. The Pilgrims call it the Hearth's
keeper. It is not; it is only warm. The Shell Stair climbs from the lake to
Stillmere, the one way back up.

- **Hearthdeep.** The bottom of the world: a still, clear lake under the fen,
  the Hearth burning under it, stone trees, gold glowing in the rock, the
  Pilgrims at Pilgrims' Rest, and the Shell Below asleep on its isle.

## Banner

Hearth rose, a shell. The Hearth's glow under the water, and the Shell Below asleep on it. The rule is in [../banners.md](../banners.md).
