## The Pilgrim

Greets you: *"Softly. It sleepeth."* Walked away from: *"Tread softly, good Tidewashed. The warm awaiteth thee."*

### Home: Hearthdeep, Pilgrims' Rest (95, 49)

- **first**
  1. Softly, I prithee. The Shell Below sleepeth on the isle yonder, upon the warm stone. Put thy hand in the water, good Tidewashed, and feel it.
  2. It keepeth the Hearth, which lieth under it, and the sisters came down from their chapel to kneel beside it.
  3. We pilgrims wait for it to wake, and it will wake. Think not on thy fears.
- **water** (heard to the end, sets **drankClear**; heard to the end the first time, gives *Hollow Water*)
  1. Drink, I prithee. 'Tis warm as a hearthstone, and naught so warm is left up there.
  2. The old man who keepeth the fire above saith not to drink it. He hath never been down.

### Dead at Hearthdeep, Pilgrims' Rest (95, 49), once **shellDead**
