## The Pilgrim

Greets you: *"Softly. It sleepeth."* Walked away from: *"Softly, going. The warm awaiteth thee."*

### Home: Hearthdeep, Pilgrims' Rest (95, 49)

- **first**
  1. Softly... It sleepeth on the isle, on the warm. Feel the water, good Tidewashed.
  2. The Hearth's keeper. The Hearth lieth under it, and the sisters came down to kneel by it.
  3. We wait for it to wake. ...It will wake. Think not on thy fears.
- **water** (heard to the end, sets **drankClear**; heard to the end the first time, gives *Hollow Water*)
  1. Drink, I prithee. It is warm. Nothing warm is left up there.
  2. The old one by the fire above saith not to. ...He hath never been down.

### Dead at Hearthdeep, Pilgrims' Rest (95, 49), once **shellDead**

Says nothing.
