# Stillmere

The canon for this land. Where it and anything else disagree, this wins; the rules and the world's story are in [../reach.md](../reach.md).

## The land

Reed and black water between the shore and the moor. It was a warm fen once,
fed from below by springs off the Hearth, and the fenfolk, the oldest people
in Aurum, laid their eggs in them. Then the Citadel's Deep Cut ran under it
into the Hearth, and the fen drained into the hole. For a season Stillmere
poured down onto the Hearth and half put it out. The Fen Sinkhole is still
drinking. The springs are cold, the fenfolk slow, and the village on the
causeway went under when the water moved and never came up: the Drowned
Village.

On the Chapel Mound over the sinkhole the Stilled built their chapel. They
are the Order's third way: warmth, they say, was the world's fever, the cold
is it breaking, and the kindest thing is to let the Hearth go out and
everything rest. They pour water into the sinkhole by the bucket. They took
the fenfolk's eggs into the chapel "to keep them", and the fenfolk guard the
chapel stair now, and carry water with the sisters.

The Shell Stair goes down from the fen to Hearthdeep, shut from above.

- **Stillmere.** Reed and black water coiling inward to the Stilled's
  chapel over the sinkhole that drinks the pools. Fenfolk guard its stair,
  their warren in the reeds; the Drowned Village lies on the causeway.

## Banner

Stilled violet, a fish over eggs. The Stilled's chapel, and the fenfolk's eggs inside it. The rule is in [../banners.md](../banners.md).

## For writers only

- The water the sinkhole drinks comes up warm at Tidewake. The Stilled do
  not know it. Every bucket they pour is the same water that gave the
  wanderer back.
- The eggs are in the chapel. Whether they will hatch in the cold is not
  said.
