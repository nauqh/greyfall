# Barrow Deeps

The canon for this land. Where it and anything else disagree, this wins; the rules and the world's story are in [../reach.md](../reach.md).

## The land

- **Barrow Deeps.** The tombs under the moor, down to the Weaver's Web. The
  Weaver keeps the deep seal and the second key. Tobin's brother runs the
  robbers' camp.
