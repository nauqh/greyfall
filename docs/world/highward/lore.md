# Highward

The canon for this land. Where it and anything else disagree, this wins; the rules and the world's story are in [../reach.md](../reach.md).

## The land

Highward is the hold over Lanternport, one of the five that broke the old
kings' crown between them. It stood between the town and the east, and fell
ward by ward, from the outside in, the way the Grey takes anything.

The bell went first. The Keepers rang it at dusk to call the Burg in to the
fires, and its roof is the highest, coldest edge Highward has, open to the
east wind. One dusk it did not ring. A warden of stone squats on the bell
roof now. With the bell silent the Lower Burg went next, its watch one man
at a time and its folk to thieving. Then the Ramparts: the garrison's
iron-shod war boar forgot who fed it, and goblins running west for
Emberwood came over the wall, and one of them is on its back. The Chapel
Ward emptied after.

The Keep alone held. Its steward barred the Keep Gate on the Burg, went out
the monks' Postern for the Emberwood road with its only key, the Ember Key,
the warmest road there was, and did not come back. Dunn of the Gate Watch heard him knock at the barred
Ember Gate and did not open. The Ember Bull has the key now. Behind the
gate the garrison is Corwin, boys drilled with broom handles, and Ansel on
the wall. The Watch Lift that served Highward's watch posts on Cloudstair
hangs chained from above.

- **Highward (Lower Burg, Ramparts, Chapel Ward, Keep).** Lanternport's own
  hold, climbed ward by ward: the Burg's fallen streets and Hale's square,
  the Ramparts and the Rampart Boar, the Chapel Ward's cloister, forge yard
  and the Stone Warden on the bell roof, and the Keep behind the Keep Gate.

## Folk

| Who | Where | Their place in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mags the Peddler | The Lower Burg, a nook under the wall | A Stiltwater trader in Sunhold's yellow who stayed when the Burg turned. Sells everything that is not a blade. |
| Hale of the Watch | The Lower Burg, the square | Held the square with six men; watched them turn after the bell stopped. Keeps the Burg's one fire. |
| Odo the Gunner | The Ramparts, the east tower | Outlived his powder. Saw the boar turn and the goblins come over the wall. |
| Brenna the Smith | The Chapel Ward, the forge yard | Highward's smith. Puts an edge on the wanderer's sword for gold and ore. |
| Sergeant Corwin | The Keep, the bailey | Led Highward's forty spears onto Kingsrest; came back alone. Hires out lancers; digs his forty out of Barrow Field once the King is dead. |
| Ansel of the Wall | The Keep, on the wall | Once a wanderer in Highward's blue with a band and a banner; stopped going out. Roused by the bull's death, goes ahead into Greyfall and dies below the Warlord's Hall unless the Warlord falls first. |
