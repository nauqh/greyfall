## Brenna the Smith

Greets you: *"Mind the sparks, love."* Walked away from: *"Come back with gold, y' hear?"*

Sells: Brenna's Edge; Keener Edge (once the Stone Warden falls); Grey-Iron Edge (once the Ember Bull falls); Banded Shield; Ansel's Edge (once **anselAhead**); Digger's Torch (once **warlordDead**).

### Home: The Chapel Ward, the Forge Yard (73, 29)

- **first**
  1. Ahh, a newcomer. Don't stand so close. Sparks don't care whose eyes they find.
  2. I'm Brenna. Smith to the Ward, for what's left of it.
  3. Off into all that grey, are y'? Then you'll want a proper edge. Bring me gold, and I'll do the smithing.
  4. There's a banded shield on the wall, too. Oak and iron. Takes the blow so y' arm doesn't.
- **ore**
  1. Mine Hills ore is soft, but honest. Brasscrag has better, under the gnomes.
  2. Go and be rude to them. They'll understand. It's how they talk to each other, bless 'em.
- **hammer**
  1. Heat, hit. Heat, hit.
  2. Same as fighting, really. Only the iron never hits back.
  3. Look after y' blade, and it'll look after you. It's all a smith can promise.
- **key** (once, after meeting, when **trollKey**)
  1. Let me see that key... Well, I'll be. The Keep's. I cut that blank m'self, years back.
  2. Smells of bull now. Wash it, would y'?
- **ansel** (once, after meeting, when **anselRoused**)
  1. Ansel came by. Paid for two edges on that old sword of his. Didn't even haggle, the old fool.
  2. Took the sword with one edge on it. Said he'd be back for the other.
- **second** (once, after meeting, when **anselAhead**)
  1. He hasn't come back for the second edge.
  2. I don't keep things for the dead, love. I sell them. Gold first.
  3. ...Oh, don't give me that look. I'm softer than I sound.

## Sergeant Corwin

Greets you: *"Halt. You're not one of mine."* Walked away from: *"Dismissed."*

### Home: The Keep, the Bailey, by the barracks (21, 29)

- **first**
  1. You're not one of mine. ...No. Mine are all gone.
  2. I am Corwin, Sergeant of the Keep. I took forty spears onto the moor once. Forty.
  3. Now I drill boys with broom handles and call it a garrison. A sorry business.
  4. Want a lance at your side? Pay, and I'll find you one who still stands.
- **drill**
  1. Point forward. Feet back.
  2. If they're charging you, you've already done it wrong. Remember that, and you might live.
- **died** (once, after meeting, when **died**)
  1. You fell. Don't tell me where.
  2. Bring me the ones still breathing. Not their names. I've names enough.
- **warlord** (once, after meeting, when **warlordDead**)
  1. Forty spears...
  2. I'll carve them into the gate, every one. Leave me to it, soldier.
- **post** (once, after meeting, when **watchmanOut**)
  1. The watchman on the Moor Gate's left his post. Good.
  2. He'll tell you he barred it on my orders. I never gave him any. ...Fear gave them.
- **ansel** (once, after meeting, when **anselRoused**)
  1. Ansel's got his sword out. Took him long enough.
  2. Took me forty men. ...Don't tell him I said so.

### At Kingsrest, Barrow Field (29, 22), once **hornedKey**

- **barrows**
  1. Ahh, you. The Barrow King is dead... The barrows are only barrows now.
  2. My forty went in after his key. So I'm digging them out. One at a time. As is proper.
- **count**
  1. Twenty-two. Dace still had his pipe on him.
  2. Holm had nothing. ...Holm always had nothing. Good lad, Holm. Good lad, Holm.
- **last**
  1. Thirty-nine...
  2. The fortieth was me, I think. By rights I ought to be down there with them.
- **done** (once, after meeting, when **warlordDead**)
  1. So. The Warlord's down. My hands stopped aching this morning.
  2. The cold's let go a little. Tell them at the gate, would you? I've no hands left for carving.
  3. Forty... and one Tidewashed. I'll make room on the gate.

  2. My forty went in after his key. So I'm digging them out. One at a time. As is proper.
- **count**
  1. Twenty-two. Dace still had his pipe on him.

## Ansel of the Wall

Greets you: *"Look up."* Walked away from: *"Go on. I'll keep watch. It's all I'm good for."*

### Home: The Keep, on the hall's terrace (43, 11)

- **first**
  1. Look up. See that glint, past the clouds?
  2. Sunhold. Gold walls, gold roofs. A city that floats on its money, and never learned to bleed.
  3. When the Grey came, it shut its gates. Not one spear. Not one prayer.
  4. ...Ansel. Of the wall, they call me now. I used to be of something else. Doesn't matter. Not now.
- **lift**
  1. The Watch Lift only comes down.
  2. Someone in Sunhold decided who gets to visit. Joyous bunch, really.
- **blue**
  1. I wore that blue once. Had a band, a banner. The fire knew my name.
  2. Then I stopped going out. ...You'd be surprised how easy it is. One day, then the next.
- **watch**
  1. Still here. Still watching. Someone ought to.
  2. Go on. I'll keep watch. It's all I'm good for.
- **lifted** (once, after meeting, when **watchLiftOpen**)
  1. So you went up. Were they kind?
  2. ...No. The sky city never is.
- **roused** (once, after meeting, when **trollKey**; heard to the end, sets **anselRoused**)
  1. The bull. You killed it. With that.
  2. ...I had a sword once. It's under the bed, I think.
  3. Maybe I've sat long enough. Don't say anything. You'll talk me out of it.

### At Greyfall, The Ashen Approach (14, 54), once **anselRoused**

- **out**
  1. Don't look at me like that. I said I'd come out, didn't I?
  2. I'd forgotten the smell of the Grey. Cold ash and old iron.
  3. Talk to me again and I'll go on up. So don't. Not unless you mean it.
- **ahead** (heard to the end, sets **anselAhead**)
  1. The Warlord's Hall is up past the High City. I'll go first, this time.
  2. I've decided to stop running from it. Let me have that. You've had all the others.

### At The Keep, on the hall's terrace (43, 11), once **anselRoused** and **warlordDead**

- **without**
  1. You went up without me. Good.
  2. I'd only have been in the way. ...I'd only have been brave. What a bloody joke.
- **wall** (heard to the end the first time, gives *Ansel's Edge*)
  1. Back on the wall. It's a good wall.
  2. Brenna was holding an edge I paid for. Here, it's yours. You can make better use of it.
  3. If she asks, tell her I sent you. She'll want it in writing. Hah...

### Dead at Greyfall, The High City (95, 27), once **anselAhead**

Says nothing.

## Mags the Peddler

Greets you: *"Hehe. A live one."* Walked away from: *"Come back with coin, dearie. As is thy wont, no?"*

Sells: Ember Shard; Smoked Haunch; Pine Resin; Prospector's Purse; Kindled Shard (once the Ember Bull falls); Salt-Glass Lantern (once **marenSails**).

### Home: The Lower Burg, Market Row, in a nook under the wall (37, 39)

- **first**
  1. Hehe. A live one! Oh, don't mind old Mags, dearie. I'm but a humble peddler.
  2. The Grey took the Burg. Not my trade, though. Nor am I so charitable as to give my wares away. Hehe.
  3. A shard of the old fire, a haunch, a pot of resin, a purse that sews itself fatter. Little things, to ease a weary traveller.
  4. One of each, mind. Keep them in thy sack, where thy hands can find them.
  5. Kill whatever sits in Emberwood, and I'll find thee a warmer shard. Hehe.
- **thieves**
  1. The thieves in the market? Burg folk, all of them.
  2. Went bad when the bell stopped. Bad for business, worse for them. More's the pity.
- **coin**
  1. Feel that coin, dearie. Warm, isn't it? Even now.
  2. That's all gold ever was. A little warm thou canst carry. Hehe.
- **rest**
  1. Hehe. Still breathing, dearie? Good.
  2. Breathing folk buy things. Is it not our sorry lot, to sup on the living?
- **fence** (once, after meeting, when **tobinDeeper**)
  1. Tobin's gone down after his brother? Hehe. Then he'll meet my best supplier.
  2. Don't ask what I buy from below, dearie. I don't. Kee hee hee...
- **ship** (once, after meeting, when **marenSails**)
  1. A ship in, and Maren off on it. Thirty years she waited. Hehe.
  2. Some of us don't wait.
  3. Stiltwater left me a crate. Glass, dearie. Salt-glass. One, mind.

## Hale of the Watch

Greets you: *"Living? Then sit a while."* Walked away from: *"Mind the bridges."*

### Home: The Lower Burg, The Burg Square, by the fire (51, 32)

- **first**
  1. Living? Oh, thank the fire. Then sit a while. ...I am Hale, of the Watch. The last of it.
  2. You came over the creek? Then you've met the bridges' keepers.
  3. I held this square with six men. The bell stopped, and they turned. One by one.
  4. The Rampart Gate's up the street. Or through the graves on the hill, if you'd sooner the dead than the dying.
- **ladder**
  1. There was a ladder down to here from the Ramparts, by the fire.
  2. Someone pulled it up. From above. ...I'd like a word with them.
- **keep** (once, after meeting, when **trollKey**)
  1. Is that the Ember Key on your belt?
  2. The Keep Gate's off the west alley. Corwin's lancers are behind it, if any are left. Mind the bridges.
- **rest**
  1. Sit. The fire's the one thing in the Burg that's still ours.
  2. I am well pleased to see you safe.

## Odo the Gunner

Greets you: *"Boar's dead, then. Good. I was running out of things to throw."* Walked away from: *"Mind the ladder. It sticks."*

### Home: The Ramparts, The East Tower (72, 12)

- **first**
  1. Odo, gunner. Two guns on that tower, mine. The goblins found my powder, and turned them on the wall.
  2. The boar was the garrison's. The smiths shod him in iron for the parade. Proud beast, he was.
  3. Then the cold got into him, and he forgot who fed him. A goblin climbed on. ...It's been riding him since.
- **ladder**
  1. There's a ladder down the south face to the Burg's square.
  2. Kick it loose. Their fire is right under it, if you need one.
- **died** (once, after meeting, when **died**)
  1. You came back. They don't, usually.
  2. The wall remembers everyone who crossed it. It just doesn't care. What a bloody joke.
- **kicked** (once, after meeting, when **ladderDown**)
  1. You found it. The square's closer than it looks. Most things are.
