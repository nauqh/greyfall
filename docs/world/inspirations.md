# Inspirations

The nearest land in Dark Souls, Elden Ring and Skyrim to each of Aurum's, matched by feel and job rather than copied. A touchstone for tone, not canon: where this and a land's `lore.md` disagree, the `lore.md` wins.

The spine is Dark Souls 1: the Hearth is its First Flame, and the Stilled are its "let the fire go out" ending held as a faith.

| Land | Dark Souls | Elden Ring | Skyrim | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Tidewake](tidewake/) | Undead Asylum (DS1) | Stranded Graveyard | Helgen escape tunnel | Tutorial cave: you wake with no name, read notes left by those before you, and fight the first boss (the Cinder Troll ≈ the Asylum Demon) |
| [Lanternport](lanternport/) | Majula (DS2), Firelink Shrine | Roundtable Hold | Riverwood | Seaside hub around the one fire that never went out |
| [Highward](highward/) | Undead Burg + Undead Parish (DS1) | Stormveil Castle | Whiterun, Dragonsreach | Castle falling ward by ward. The stone warden on the bell roof ≈ the Bell Gargoyles, the boar on the ramparts ≈ the Parish boar |
| [Emberwood](emberwood/) | Demon Ruins (DS1), loosely | none close | Eastmarch hot springs | Wood over warm ground. None of the three is the real match: Bloodborne's Forbidden Woods is (a village that worships what it feeds, torchlight in the dark, ambush from the houses, a sprawl with a shortcut home), with Sekiro's Mibu Village for folk held in place by what they drink, here the warmth. The horned Ember Bull ≈ the Taurus and Capra Demons |
| [Brasscrag](brasscrag/) | Earthen Peak (DS2) | Grand Lift of Dectus | Dwemer ruins, Markarth | A small craft-folk's mine of wheels, lifts and gears. Old Bamboo at his wheel ≈ Earthen Peak's windmill |
| [Stiltwater](stiltwater/) | No-Man's Wharf (DS2) | none close | Riften | Boardwalks over water, smugglers below, militia on someone's pay (Riften's Ratway and Thieves Guild) |
| [Kingsrest](kingsrest/) | Catacombs (DS1) | Weeping Peninsula catacombs | Nordic barrows, Hjaalmarch | A barrow moor whose dead walk their own roads. Tolkien's Barrow-downs is the deeper root |
| [Graveroad](graveroad/) | Catacombs to Tomb of the Giants, Quelaag's Domain | Siofra River | Blackreach | Grave-roads that run on into an older dark. The Weaver ≈ Quelaag |
| [Stillmere](stillmere/) | Farron Keep (DS3), Blighttown | Lake of Rot | Hjaalmarch, the Argonians' eggs and the Hist | A drained fen and a cult that wants the fire out (≈ Kaathe's Age of Dark) |
| [Greyfall](greyfall/) | Iron Keep and the Old Iron King (DS2) | Caelid, Castle Redmane | Windhelm | A rich citadel ruined by its lord's greed over a rot below. The Deep Cut ≈ Izalith's flame gone wrong |
| [Cloudstair](cloudstair/) | Archdragon Peak (DS3), loosely | Haligtree branches, loosely | none | None of the three has drifting isles; Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom's Sky Islands is the real match |
| [Sunhold](sunhold/) | Anor Londo (DS1) | Leyndell | Solitude | A golden city above it all, its gate shut on the world below. The Sun Sentinel ≈ Anor Londo's Sentinels |
| [Underwell](underwell/) | The Depths + New Londo Ruins (DS1) | Subterranean Shunning-Grounds | the Ratway | Cisterns and catacombs under the hub, a chapel half under water |
| [Hearthdeep](hearthdeep/) | Ash Lake (DS1) | Nokron, Siofra | Blackreach | The bottom of the world: stone trees, still water, something ancient asleep (the Shell Below ≈ the Everlasting Dragon) |

The closest one-to-one matches are Highward and the Undead Burg and Parish, Sunhold and Anor Londo, Hearthdeep and Ash Lake, and Stiltwater and Riften.
