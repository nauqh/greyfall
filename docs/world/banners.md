# Banners

A banner for each land, hung beside its name in the bonfire list. Each
land's own banner is under Banner in its `lore.md`; the colours and charges
as drawn are in `apps/web/src/app/atlas/banners.ts`.

## The rule

- **Every land has its own colour**, taken from the land itself: its
  ground, its water, the thing it burns or keeps. No two lands share one.
  The canon fixes two: Highward's blue ("I wore that blue once") and
  Greyfall's grey.
- **The charge is what the land is known for**, one thing its folk would
  point at. A charge is a silhouette from game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0, credit
  each artist in the README).
- **The Grey takes colour first** ("banners go grey first"), so Greyfall's
  cloth is the one already grey, and no one remembers what the Citadel flew.
- Banners are cloth in menus, not units or buildings: rule 10's five
  people colours still hold for everything drawn in the field.
