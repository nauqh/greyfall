# Greyfall sound

Every sound the game plays, named for what it does in `apps/web/src/app/sound.ts`.

## Made for Greyfall

| File | How |
|---|---|
| `world/dash.ogg` | the onward gust quickened to 0.42 s: the same wind, peaking 130 ms in and gone as the roll lands |
| `world/onward.ogg` | wind noise through a band-pass swept 180-600 Hz, swelling in and falling away |
| `world/arrive.ogg` | Kenney `doorClose_4` and `bong_001` (Interface Sounds) pitched down, with a long echo |
| `world/lit.ogg` | a low noise whoomp, a stretch of the Pixabay fire clip, and a low fifth (98/147/196 Hz) rung out |
| `world/crackle.ogg` | the Pixabay fire clip's steady 5-17 s, its seam crossfaded into a loop |
| `troll/growl.ogg` | troll-roars' low roar (5.4-6.3 s) slowed to 0.8, darkened, with a cavern echo |
| `troll/fall.ogg` | wind noise through a band-pass swept 140-660 Hz, swelling to the moment of impact |
| `troll/land.ogg` | hard-heavy-impact with a cavern echo and rubble patter off the roof |
| `troll/roar.ogg` | troll-roars' loudest roar (1.9-2.9 s) with a short cavern tail |
| `troll/tell-slam.ogg` | beast-growling's steady middle (0.75-1.85 s) |
| `troll/tell-sweep.ogg`, `troll/tell-charge.ogg`, `troll/tell-toss.ogg` | troll-roars' 0.1 s, 3.6 s and 9.4 s roars, a short cavern tail on each |
| `troll/slam.ogg` | boulder-impact's first 1.3 s with a cavern tail |
| `troll/rock.ogg` | boulder-impact pitched up a quarter, cut to a crunch |
| `troll/step.ogg` | giant-walking's first footfall |
| `troll/charge.ogg` | that footfall four times, quickening |
| `troll/dazed.ogg` | beast-growling's grumbling tail (2.3-4.4 s) slowed to 0.85 and darkened |
| `music/boss.ogg` | sport-intense-metal's 24.1-86.4 s, a 24-bar stretch whose seam matches, levelled 18 dB down under the effects |
| `ui/herald.ogg` | a noise swell rising to the banner's full light, into a low D chord (73-294 Hz) rung long, for a boss fallen or a thing gained |
| `music/landing.ogg` | medieval-ambient's first 200 s, its last 3 s crossfaded into its start, raised 6 dB to -22 dB RMS |
| `ui/click.flac` | our own |
| `music/theme.mp3` | our own |
