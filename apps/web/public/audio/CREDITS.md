# Greyfall sound

Every sound the game plays, named for what it does in `apps/web/src/app/sound.ts`.

## Made for Greyfall

| File | How |
|---|---|
| `onward.ogg` | wind noise through a band-pass swept 180-600 Hz, swelling in and falling away |
| `arrive.ogg` | Kenney `doorClose_4` and `bong_001` pitched down, with a long echo |
| `lit.ogg` | a low noise whoomp, a stretch of the Pixabay fire clip, and a low fifth (98/147/196 Hz) rung out |
| `crackle.ogg` | the Pixabay fire clip's steady 5-17 s, its seam crossfaded into a loop |
| `click.flac` | our own |
| `theme.mp3` | our own |
